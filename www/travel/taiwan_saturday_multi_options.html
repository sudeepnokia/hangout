Option 1: TSMC Museum of Innovation

Vibe: Prestigious & High-Tech Local Tour

Explore the highly prestigious semiconductor museum of the world's most critical tech company, located right in your hotel's neighborhood (Hsinchu Science Park).

⚠️ Strict Booking Required: Admission is free, but advance online booking is mandatory. Reserve slots immediately on tsmcmoi.com.

05:00 PM – 06:00 PM Taxi to Hsinchu HSR (10 mins). Board the 5:30 PM bullet train to Taipei, arriving by 6:00 PM.
TSMC Museum Map

Option 2: Syntrend Creative Park

Vibe: Futuristic Geek Heaven & Gadgets

Explore a massive 12-story high-tech shopping dome in Taipei, featuring concept flagships, virtual reality setups, audio flagships, and anime figure halls.

🔓 No Reservations Needed: Fully open, fully air-conditioned, and open until 10:00 PM on Saturdays.

04:45 PM – 06:00 PM Syntrend Tech Tour: Explore the gaming floor, flagship cameras, and massive anime/Marvel collectible statues. Fully flat with escalators.

Option 3: CKS Memorial Hall

Vibe: Grand Political Monument & Honor Ceremony

Stroll the grand paved plazas of Liberty Square. Witness the solemn and highly synchronized Changing of the Guard ceremony at 5:00 PM (the final one of the day), held completely step-free on the outdoor Democracy Boulevard.

⚠️ Outdoor Relocation: The guard ceremony takes place hourly from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. It is now held entirely outdoors on Democracy Boulevard (100% step-free viewing) and is canceled during heavy rain.
03:35 PM – 04:35 PM Taxi to Hsinchu HSR (3 mins). Board bullet train to Taipei. Take a direct 10-minute taxi straight to Liberty Square.

05:15 PM – 05:35 PM Grab a direct 12-minute taxi straight from CKS to Taipei 101 to meet the group.

Survival Intel ▼

Saturday Travel Intelligence

  • EasyCard is Vital: Keep your EasyCard topped up. It covers city buses, subways, local trains, and can be used for payments at 7-Eleven, FamilyMart, and Starbucks.
  • No Tipping Policy: Tipping is not customary in Taiwan. A 10% service charge is automatically added to high-end dining establishments like hotel and resort restaurants.
  • Cash is King at Markets: While hotels, HSR, and large malls accept credit cards, night markets and street food stalls exclusively accept local cash (TWD).
  • Subway Cleanliness: Eating, drinking, or chewing gum is strictly prohibited inside the subways and station gates. Violators face heavy fines.

Vegetarian Dining Guarantee

Taiwan is highly vegetarian-friendly. Show this card on your phone to any local food vendor to guarantee a strictly meat-free dish:

我吃素，不吃肉、魚、海鮮、明膠、豬油。

(I am vegetarian; I do not eat meat, fish, seafood, gelatin, or lard.)
