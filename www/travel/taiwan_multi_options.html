Taiwan Survival Intelligence

  • EasyCard is Vital: Keep your EasyCard topped up. It covers city subways, local trains, and can be used for payments at 7-Eleven, FamilyMart, and Starbucks.
  • No Tipping Policy: Tipping is not customary in Taiwan. A 10% service charge is automatically added to high-end dining establishments like hotel and resort restaurants.
  • Cash is King at Markets: While hotels, HSR, and large malls accept credit cards, night markets and street food stalls exclusively accept local cash (TWD).
  • Subway Cleanliness: Eating, drinking, or chewing gum is strictly prohibited inside the subways and station gates. Violators face heavy fines.
