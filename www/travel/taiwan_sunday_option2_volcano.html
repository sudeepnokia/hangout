09:45 AM – 11:30 AM Scenic Highlight

Lush Volcanic Valleys: Erziping Forest Trail

Drive up Yangmingshan to the gorgeous **Erziping Trail**, the national park's premier fully paved, level pathway. Stroll through the lush, misty forest, listening to bird calls and mountain streams. The pathway leads to a stunning alpine lake surrounded by three towering volcanic peaks, completely free of any steps or elevation hurdles.

11:30 AM – 12:30 PM Geology

Roaring Volcanic Geysers: Xiaoyoukeng Fumaroles

Drive to the dramatic post-volcanic landscape of **Xiaoyoukeng**. Witness massive, active steam vents (fumaroles) shooting mineral-rich steam directly out of the mountain. Admire boiling mud pools, brilliant yellow sulfur crystals, and panoramic valley views. The primary observation deck is flat and sits directly next to the parking lot.

Explore **Geothermal Valley (Hell Valley)**. Stroll along the completely flat, newly upgraded wooden boardwalk wrapped around a spectacular, steaming lake of jade-green geothermal water. Expedition intelligence

  • EasyCard is Vital: Keep your EasyCard topped up. It covers city buses, subways, local trains, and can be used for payments at 7-Eleven, FamilyMart, and Starbucks.
  • No Tipping Policy: Tipping is not customary in Taiwan. A 10% service charge is automatically added to high-end dining establishments like hotel and resort restaurants.
  • Convenience Stores: FamilyMart and 7-Eleven throughout the Beitou and Yangmingshan area are excellent for hot beverages, fresh snacks, ATMs, and immediate EasyCard top-ups.
  • Climate & Gear: Yangmingshan mountains are cooler and mistier than Hsinchu. A light windbreaker/jacket is highly recommended.

Vegetarian Dining Guarantee

Taiwan is highly vegetarian-friendly. Show this card on your phone to any local food vendor to guarantee a strictly meat-free dish:

我吃素，不吃肉、魚、海鮮、明膠、豬油。

(I am vegetarian; I do not eat meat, fish, seafood, gelatin, or lard.)
